# Twitter Build-in-Public Plan — KWASU Portal

**Posting frequency:** 3–4× per week
**Duration:** 8 weeks to first demo
**Content types:** 5 formats in rotation

---

## Week 1 — The Origin Thread

> Anchor everything that follows.

**Intro thread (do this first)**

> "I'm rebuilding my university's student portal from scratch. Here's why the current one is a disaster — and what I'm replacing it with. 🧵"

**Before/after post**

> Side-by-side screenshot of the current KWASU portal vs your first wireframe/mockup. No caption needed — the contrast does the talking.

> 💡 Pin the intro thread to your profile immediately. Everyone who finds you later will see it first.

---

## Weeks 2–4 — Building in Public

> Show the work, 3–4× per week.

**Screen recording**

> 30s video of something working — real-time notification, role switch, timetable render. No voiceover needed.

**Problem post**

> "Why does every Nigerian uni portal show the same cluttered mess to students AND admins? Fixing that."

**Small win**

> "Role-based dashboard is live. Students see X, lecturers see Y, admin sees Z. One codebase."

**Decision post**

> "Chose Turborepo over a single Next.js app. Here's why for a project with 3 different user types..."

> 💡 Mix kwasu-portal updates in here too — 1 in every 4 posts. Keeps your profile cohesive: "builder solving Nigerian edu problems."

---

## Weeks 5–7 — Going Deeper

> Build credibility with substance.

**Mini-thread (2–3 tweets)**

> "3 things I learned building a monorepo for a university system:" — pick something genuinely interesting from the build. Developers will retweet this.

**Community post**

> Ask your audience something. "What feature would actually make you use your uni portal?" — engagement + real feedback + algorithm boost.

**kwasu-portal crossover**

> "Both projects are solving the same root problem — Nigerian universities are underserved by software. One for attendance, one for everything else."

---

## Week 8 — The Demo Drop

> Biggest post yet.

**Demo thread**

> "8 weeks ago I started rebuilding my university's portal. Here's what it looks like today — live demo link inside. 🧵"

> 💡 This is the post to put effort into. Record a proper walkthrough video (1–2 min), include the live URL, and tag a few Nigerian tech accounts. Everything before this was building an audience for this moment.

---

## Content Format Cheatsheet

| Format                        | When to use                                                 |
| ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Screen recordings**         | Highest engagement — post whenever something works visually |
| **Problem/frustration posts** | Relatable, shareable, great for reach                       |
| **Technical decisions**       | Attracts developer audience, builds credibility             |
| **Questions to audience**     | Boosts engagement, gives you real feedback                  |
| **Milestone/win posts**       | Celebrate progress, keeps momentum visible                  |
